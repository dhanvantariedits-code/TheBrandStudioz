01 / About

A little about
the editor.

Creative editing with a focus on pacing, visual storytelling, clean motion and content that keeps people watching.

I'm a fresher Video Editor & Motion Designer building my craft through practical projects and creative experimentation.

I enjoy turning raw footage into polished content that feels intentional — from fast-paced social edits and talking-head videos to long-form content, podcasts and motion graphics.

My editing style focuses on strong pacing, clean transitions, engaging captions, visual rhythm and motion that supports the story instead of distracting from it.

I'm continuously learning, experimenting and refining my workflow across different types of content.
